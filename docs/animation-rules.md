# NetBattle animation rules

The one copy of the rules every animation and movement agent reads first.
Decisions marked (user) were stated by the project owner and are not open
for change without asking.

## Fight design (user)

- Traffic drives frequency, power and defence. It never drives speed.
- The leader chases. The outclassed side barely attacks and gets dodged in slow motion.
- No jumps (`ALLOW_JUMPS = false`).
- Hover gives slow motion. A 2 s hover opens the info panel.
- Strikes land on the guard or the body. Never in the air, never through the body.
- Pixel art with a black outline and flat 3-tone shading. No near-duplicate consecutive frames.

## Clip rules

- A new clip starts and ends on the reference stance: feet x 38 to 50 (red) or 39 to 48 (blue) on the 88 px canvas. Otherwise the hand-over between clips pops.
- Frames that matter are chosen in `CLIP_FRAMES` in `src/main.js`, per fighter. Raw PixelLab frames hold the extended limb too long.
- Red's feet are thickened on import (`tools/thicken_feet.py`).
- Re-import wipes `src/sprites/<key>/` and rebuilds it: `powershell -File tools/import_pixellab.ps1 -Key down|up -CharacterId <id>`.
- The step clips (`stepF`, `stepB`, `stepClose`) are generated by interpolation. Only stepClose frames 1, 2 and 8 are used; frames 3 to 7 drift.
- PixelLab animation uses v3 interpolation with `custom_start_frame_url` and `end_frame_url`.

## Movement rules

- Fighters move only by push-steps (widen clip, then rear foot follows). They run only when far apart.
- One foot stays planted in every frame. If both feet move, the fighter slides.
- No stepping while the opponent attacks. No striking a stepping or skidding opponent (`|vx| > 15`).
- A 0.3 to 0.9 s rest between footwork steps prevents in and out deadlocks.
- Sprite positions are in world px. Place sprites from `me.x`, never from the procedural skeleton hip, which lunges.

## Strike rules

- `predictPast` predicts where a strike lands from the drawn pose. The target is 4 px past the guard (`LAND_AT`), window plus or minus 12 px, plus up to 16 px of inching in the wind-up.
- Low kicks aim at the opponent's lead toe.
- Even exchanges: evasions become blocks. Only an outclassed attacker gets dodged. Ducks only under head kicks from range.
- Defences are cued 0.3 s before impact. Strikes aim where a blocking guard will be at impact.
- Combos follow whatever reaches. A clean block opens a counter.

## Test modes and targets

Set `NETBATTLE_SHOWCASE` before starting the app (`npx tauri dev`, output to a log file):

| Value | What it does |
|---|---|
| `1` | Every move in a fixed order, for recording |
| `fight` | The real fight AI with fake traffic. Logs STRIKE, IMPACT, TRACE and BOTH |
| `contact` | Each strike starts at contact distance and freezes 0.5 s at impact |

Checks, run on a 3 minute `fight` log:

| Script | Reports |
|---|---|
| `tools/combo_check.py` | Combo length, variety, pauses, strikes landing bad |
| `tools/trace_check.py` | Feet that slide, guard glides |
| `tools/motion_check.py`, `overlap_check.py` | Body jumps and overlap in a recording |
| `tools/impact_sheet.py` | Pictures of impacts |
| `tools/audit_clips.py`, `foot_track.py`, `analyze_showcase.py`, `zoom_sheet.py` | Clip and recording audits |

Targets for a 3 minute fight:

- 40% or more of attacks have 2 or more strikes.
- No strike is more than 25% of one fighter's strikes.
- 8 or more different strikes per fighter.
- Strikes landing in the air or through the body: 5% or fewer (excluding intended dodges).
- No pause between strikes over 4 s.
- Zero feet slides in step, guard and block. Zero `ERROR` lines.
- CPU on the release build: 34% of one core or less.
